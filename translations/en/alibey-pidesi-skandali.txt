THE ALIBEY PIDE SCANDAL

Salim Sinan Tuncer, the famous fashion personality and owner of the rights to the name Pornstar Martini, was spotted leaving Salon Figaro Wedding Hall, an iconic venue in Hürriyet Mahallesi. Clearly delighted that his close friend O.E., also known as the "Bull of Allston," had gotten married, Tuncer spoke to Allston Son Dakika's cameras.

AN EXTRAORDINARY WEDDING

Outside the hall, Tuncer briefly expressed his satisfaction with the event:

"They put on a truly magnificent reception. May their happiness last, and may they grow old together."

The course of events reportedly changed after that statement. According to sources, Mr. Tuncer disappeared shortly after an argument inside the hall.

The main course, "Alibey Pide," allegedly became a point of disagreement between the families throughout the evening. Since the name "Alibey" appeared to refer to an unidentified person, someone from the bride's side demanded, "Who the f*** is Ali?" Mr. Tuncer responded, "Şehirlerarasıotobüsterminaliocza," an incomprehensible string of words. The answer inflamed the dispute.

RELATIVES GREW ANGRY; THE PIDE WENT COLD

A brief silence fell over the hall. Several relatives on the bride's side were then reportedly upset by Tuncer's answer.

"Only a scoundrel would give these people a daughter in marriage," members of the bride's family said. The groom's side tried to calm them and find Mr. Tuncer to clear things up, but witnesses said he had vanished immediately after his reply.

It remains unknown whether the families subsequently disagreed about serving the Alibey Pide at all.
